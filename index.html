Attendance by face, stored as numbers

Each face becomes a 128-number vector (an embedding). A new face is compared with the saved vectors by distance. If it is close enough, the person is marked present and their name and time are saved.

How it works

1. Capture

The phone or laptop camera sees the face in the browser.

2. Embed

A model turns the face into a 128-D vector. The background is ignored.

3. Match

The closest saved vector wins if its distance is below the threshold.

4. Record

Name, roll number, session, date and time are saved once per day.
