Troubleshooting

The camera does not open

Allow camera access in the browser address bar. The page must be opened with https:// or localhost, not by double-clicking the file. Close other apps that use the camera.

"Could not load the face models"

The face models download from the internet the first time. Check your connection and reload the page.

It does not recognise me

Use good front light, face the camera and stay close. Register again with 5 clear samples. You can raise the threshold slightly in Admin.

It marks the wrong person

Lower the threshold or raise the margin in Admin, and register each person with more varied samples.

My data disappeared

Data is saved in this browser only. Clearing browser data or using a different browser or device removes it. Use Admin → Export backup regularly.
