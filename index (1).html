Attendance by face, stored as numbers

Each face becomes a 128-number vector (an embedding). A new face is compared with saved vectors by Euclidean distance. If it is close enough, attendance is marked and the person's name is saved.

1. Capture

The phone or laptop camera captures the face in the browser.

2. Embed

A model turns the face into a 128-D vector. Background is ignored.

3. Match

The closest saved vector wins if its distance is below the threshold.

4. Record

Name, roll number, date and time are stored once per day.
